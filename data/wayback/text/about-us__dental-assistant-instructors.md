# Instructors

### Dr. Roger Humphreys

Founder/Director/Instructor

Dr. Humphreys has practiced general dentistry in Colorado for 36 years.  He completed his undergraduates’ studies at the University of Nebraska in May 1975. He then entered dental college at the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry, and received his DDS degree in May 1979.

In Dr. Humphreys 36 years of dentistry he has gained experience in all areas of general dentistry including oral surgery, implants, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry and periodontics. He owns a 5 operatory dental practice in Colorado Springs, Colorado with state of the art equipment which enables a world class dental assistant teaching facility.

Dr. Humphreys is also a member of American Dental Association, Colorado Dental Association and Colorado Springs Dental Society.

### Cindy Humphreys D.A.

Administrative Director /Instructor

Cindy has over 30 years of dental assisting experience. She received her certificate of Dental Assisting in 1985 and has continued her education to expanded duties in 1988.

Cindy has extensive knowledge in Soft Dent practice management software and training. Cindy is exceptionally skilled in all financial and managerial aspects of operating and maintaining a private dental practice. She is compassionate and a natural teacher.

### Beverly D.A.

Student Coordinator/ Instructor

Bev is one of our knowledgeable administrative staff. She started in dentistry over 30 years ago. Bev grew up in Texas and moved to Colorado, where she fell in love with the outdoors. She is the mother of one amazing daughter and a very special grandson.

Bev has always had a passion for dentistry and enjoys all the relationships she forms with patients. Her wide range of skills makes her a perfect fit in our administrative department helping our patients with an enjoyable dental experience. When she is not at work, she loves the outdoors, antiques and cooking.

### Samantha D.A.

Instructor

Samantha, one of our wonderful Expanded Functions Dental Assistants, has worked with Dr. Humphreys since 2013. She is a Texas native and moved to Colorado as a teenager with her family. Samantha’s passion and love for beautiful smiles made her pursue her dental assisting certification at Pikes Peak Community College in 2012; where she graduated with honors. She then went on to further her education and graduated as an Expanded Functions Dental Assistant (EFDA).

Samantha is putting her skills to good use to be more involved with patients and watch them transform their existing smiles into the smiles of their dreams. Her enthusiasm for learning and expanding her knowledge is always important to her.

Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with friends and family. When she is not venturing outdoors, she challenges herself with a new craft or a baking recipe from Pinterest.

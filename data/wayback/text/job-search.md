# Lifetime Job Placement Assistance

American Institute of Dental Assisting offers Lifetime Job Placement Assistance. Helping our students find gainful employment after graduation is our number one goal! We make great effort to assist our students in [https://dentalassistantco.com/] finding employment in the dental office and environment that suits them best. Many of our graduates find employment with the dental practice where they spend their externship.

Our students know how to actually perform basic assisting skills- because they have already learned and performed these skills in the school's live clinics. Employing dentists look for students who have actually performed clinical skills on live patients.

Our placement assistance includes: preparation of a resume, assistance writing cover letters, direct placement of job leads, mock interviews, clinical reviews and  internship/externship opportunities. Our program provides a presentation which discusses occupational research, interview tips, techniques and job seeking strategies.

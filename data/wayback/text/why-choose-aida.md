# Why Choose AIDA

## See What Makes AIDA the #1 Choice for

Dental Assistant Career Training

Here are just a few of the reasons why you should sign-up for our program today!

Hands-on training in a real dental clinic

True hands-on experience assisting a real dentist treating real patients. Our live patient clinics offer a one of a kind experience for dental assistant students that can’t be simulated by dummies and textbooks.

Training in Digital Radiography (the future of dentistry)

AIDA students learn to take real digital x-rays and pantographs. Other dental assisting schools don’t have state-of-the-art digital radiology equipment and only teach outdated analog x-ray techniques.

Hands-on training in Soft Dent - the most widely used software in dentistry

Most other dental assisting schools don’t have Soft Dent and just teach basic computer skills, not specific to dentistry. AIDA has a multi-station dental software laboratory where students are taught to use computers and Soft Dent.

Outstanding staff and faculty - learn directly from experienced dental professionals

All of our instructors have a minimum of 10 years assisting experience and are required to be presently working full-time in dentistry.

Best dental assisting school in Colorado

AIDA curriculum has been honored as best of the best. Because it involves working chairside with a licensed Colorado dentist you are receiving actual hands on learning and training.

School is owned, guided and directed by a licensed Colorado State dentist

AIDA is a private Colorado dental assistant school that is not owned by a large corporation but by a local dentist with over 36 years of dental experience. Dental assisting is the only course taught and it isn’t mixed with many other areas of study. We’re fully focused on delivering the best dental assisting education possible to help others help themselves and those around them.

Affordable finance options are available

Call our student recruiter today at [tel:(719) 297-5340] (719) 297-5340 to learn about our many different affordable finance options!

Total course completed in just 13 weeks!

With 36 years as a dentist, Dr. Humphreys is uniquely qualified to know what knowledge and skills dental employers are looking for in a good entry-level dental assistant. Students are only taught the necessary skills with no wasting time learning “fluff” and things which won’t be required in dental assisting. Your time is precious — don’t waste it!

Choose the dental assisting school that gives you more bang for your buck and prepares you better for your chosen profession as an Colorado dental assistant. The better prepared and trained you are, the better chance for employment in a difficult economic environment.

We’re here to help!

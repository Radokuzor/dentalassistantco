# 02 — Comparable Sites to Model

We are building an **independent Colorado dental-assistant career hub** that earns money from lead gen, jobs,
affiliates/ads, and our own prep products. No single competitor does all four, so each part of the site borrows from a
different model.

## A. Direct competitors: Colorado dental-assistant schools (they're also potential lead buyers)
| Site | Operator | Offer | What to copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| coloradospringsdentalassistant.com | Zollege (network) | 12 weeks, Saturdays 1:30–6pm, from $3,350, payment plans from $100/wk | Homepage order: event banner, lead form, overview, 4-step process, outcomes, skills, Google reviews (4.9★), 6 reasons, **free course guide download**, contact. Form asks for **start date** and **age/diploma** qualifiers. |
| denverdentalassistant.com | Zollege | $3,250 paid in full | Salary guide blog ("Dental Assistant Salary in Colorado: Denver Guide 2026"): the exact kind of query we should own |
| coloradodentalassistingschool.com | Independent | 10 weeks, Saturdays or Tue/Thu evenings, Aurora and Colorado Springs, $3,995 | Lead magnet: "Free Report: The 5 (Must Know) Reasons…", upcoming-classes table, "Real Patients / Real Dentists / Real Assistants" pillars |
| academyfordentalassistingcareers.com | Independent | — | Another school to list and pitch |
| dentalassistantschoolsaz.com | AIDA Arizona | 1 day/week × 13 weeks | Reference only. **Do not imitate.** |

**Takeaway:** Colorado schools cost about $3,250–$5,995 and run 10–13 weeks on weekends or evenings. A neutral
comparison page ("Colorado dental assistant schools compared: cost, schedule, radiology") would outrank each school's
own page for comparison queries. It's also our sales pitch to those schools for paid lead programs.

| Site | Model | What to copy |

| Dental assisting prep sellers (e.g., practice-test sites) | Paid practice exams, flashcards | Our own **"Colorado Radiology (RHS) Prep Pack"** as a digital product |
| Scrubs/loupes review blogs | Amazon and brand affiliates | "Best scrubs for dental assistants" and "first-week kit" posts |

## E. UX benchmarks to beat
- **Speed:** most competitors are WordPress page builders. A static Next.js site should load in under 1s LCP on mobile.
- **Honesty:** competitors hide tuition. We publish a **transparent cost table**, which builds trust and wins "how much" queries.
- **Interactive tools:** none of them offer a salary calculator, a "which path is right for me" quiz, or a requirements
  checker. These are cheap to build and good for links and engagement.

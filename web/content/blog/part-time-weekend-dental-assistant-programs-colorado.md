Most people who want to become dental assistants can't quit their job to go to school. Fortunately, Colorado has several programs built around that. Here's every part-time format we track, with the facts each school publishes.

## Saturday programs

**Best for:** people with a Monday-to-Friday job and weekend childcare.

You give up one day a week for about three months. Saturdays are long, usually lecture plus hands-on lab. The advantage is that nothing about your weekday routine has to change except for studying.

Both Colorado Springs schools and the Aurora campus of Colorado Dental Assisting School offer Saturdays, and Denver Dental Assistant School runs a weekend format.

## Weeknight programs

**Best for:** people whose weekends are already taken (retail or restaurant jobs, weekend custody) or who'd rather keep Saturdays free.

Colorado Dental Assisting School's Tuesday/Thursday evening option covers the same program in two shorter sessions a week. It's more tiring on those days, but your weekends stay free.

## Hybrid programs

**Best for:** self-motivated learners, especially in Northern Colorado.

The Academy for Dental Assisting Careers combines online coursework with hands-on training inside working dental practices in Longmont and Greeley. The online part fits around your life. The practice hours still need scheduling, so ask when they happen. [What to know about online options](/blog/can-become-dental-assistant-online/).

## Questions to ask any part-time program

1. **Does it include radiography training that qualifies me for x-rays in Colorado?** This is the most important one ([Colorado's rules](/requirements/)).
2. **How many hands-on hours are there, and on what?** Typodonts, classmates, real patients?
3. **Are there externship or clinical hours outside class time?** When, and who arranges them?
4. **What's included in the price?** Books, scrubs, CPR, exam fees, supplies.
5. **Is there a payment plan?** And a discount for paying in full?
6. **What happens if I miss a class?** Make-up options matter for working parents.
7. **Is the school approved to operate in Colorado?** You can check with the state higher education department ([how](/resources/#cdhe)).

## Making it work

Part-time programs are short but intense. Plan childcare, tell your household what the next three months look like, and study in short daily bursts rather than one big weekly session.
